## Disposition and next controlled step

The new response uses the separate source-status and preservation fields, but
never chooses optional or unresolved for a listed commitment. Uncertainty is
still recorded in the item-level unresolved lists. This distribution alone
cannot establish overannotation: annotators may enumerate different sets of
commitments. It also cannot establish that the clarification fixed calibration.

Keep P02, P05 and P06 preservation obligations open. Preserve ordinary facts
as provisionally compatible, not adjudicated gold. Do not rewrite old raw
answers or silently migrate their required flags into this schema. No numerical
agreement, timing or architectural superiority claim follows.

Next collect fresh responses under the same frozen 2.1 instructions, then
align preservation objects before comparing decisions. In particular compare
preserving a cue with separately requiring an inference to remain available.
Use independently assessed realizations that retain the cue but alter the
inference, if such realizations can be justified, to test whether those are
distinct obligations. The research priority remains calibration and adjudication
before expanding FoM semantics or replacing the benchmark gold.
